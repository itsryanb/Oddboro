- [office] **Decision (2026-11-08): `npm run setup` asks nothing. It checks the computer and Node, starts the office as a login service and opens the browser. There a new owner's first visit runs six cards over their empty lot (this computer, their name, Your AI, the Office Manager, the space, the clock), then they watch the office go up and their manager arrive by bus. No card asks about error reports or a New Altitude sign-in: the owner finds both in Setup. The space card offers Main Street as the quick default and a drawn office as custom (1-2 minutes). A drawn office goes up only once every piece is drawn, with a card showing the layout being built meanwhile. The office moves in when the drawing is done, with no second yes.** The browser can fix a missing Claude Code install or sign-in with a button, so the terminal keeps only what the browser cannot run without. The drawing starts when the owner describes the space, so most of it is done by the time they open the office. Raising it on library stand-ins and swapping the drawn pieces in afterwards showed the owner an office that changed under them. Asking about reports and the sign-in up front slowed a new owner on the way to a working office, and the owner ruled so. Supersedes the 2026-10-30 ruling that setup asks the name, time zone and database in the terminal, and the 2026-11-05 ruling that the first visit asks about error reports and offers the sign-in; Your AI follows the 2026-11-05 ruling on the tool the office runs on. Code in `packages/ledger/src/setup.ts` and `apps/office/src/lib/ui/onboarding/`.
